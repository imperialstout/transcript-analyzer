# Custom Vocabulary

**Purpose:** A list of program-specific terms — names, acronyms, product names, and
concepts — that your transcription tool frequently misspells or mangles.
Feed this list to your transcription tool's custom vocabulary feature (if it has one)
to improve accuracy. It is also read by the analyzer to help Claude normalize
non-standard spellings in transcript text.

**Maintenance:** Add new terms as they enter the program and get mishandled at least
twice. Remove names of people who leave the program. Review monthly.

**Format:** One term per line inside each fenced block. Headers below are for human
reading only — remove them if your transcription tool requires a flat list.
